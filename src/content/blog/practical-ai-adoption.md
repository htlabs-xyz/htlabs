Teams often ask "where should we apply AI?" before asking "what is the most expensive
manual process we run today?" Start with the second question.

## A simple framework

1. **Find the pain** — high-volume, rule-heavy, error-prone work.
2. **Instrument first** — measure baseline cost, time, error rate.
3. **Ship narrow** — one workflow, one team, one quarter.
4. **Expand only after measurement** — improvement must be visible in metrics.

## What we avoid

Big-bang transformations. Anything that needs more than 90 days to show value.
Solutions in search of a problem.
