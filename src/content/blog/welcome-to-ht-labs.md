Most AI projects never reach production. At HT Labs, we focus on the boring middle —
the integration work, the observability, the edge cases — that turns a model into a
working system. This post introduces our point of view and what to expect from us.

## Why production matters

A demo that works once is not a system. We optimize for what runs reliably on
Monday morning, six months from now, with new data and new users.

## What we're working on

Internal tooling for evaluation pipelines, agent orchestration patterns, and a small
set of in-house products that exercise everything we recommend to clients.
